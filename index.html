How we help

The EMBED framework

Real service improvements, without disrupting your day-to-day operations.

E

Evaluate

Examining the effectiveness of people, processes and platforms, including the AI tools you already have, using metrics such as queries by purchase phase, time to resolution and contact rates.

M

Map

Identifying every touchpoint of the end-to-end customer journey, deciding where to optimise and where AI can help, then measuring conversions.

B

Benchmark

Understanding how you compare, setting up dashboards, revealing focus areas, and tracking and improving NPS and CSAT scores.

E

Engage

Creating cohesion and gaining buy-in from your people, setting priorities and delivering success as a team.

D

Deliver

Quick wins and lasting change, with due regard to budgets, impact versus effort, and continual measurement and improvement.

Connect Stripe and take payment before booking

Losing money to no-shows and last-minute cancellations? Take payment when people book. On a paid Google plan, Google Calendar can do it through Stripe, with no extra booking tool.

Connect Stripe to Google Calendar, set the price and write a clear cancellation policy. Your AI chat coaches you through it, at your pace.

Get the instructions

Who it's for

You already take bookings in Google Calendar and want to:

  • Take card payment before the booking goes through
  • Set a clear cancellation and refund policy, so no-shows still pay

Still on Calendly? Start with Swap Calendly for Google Calendar bookings, then come back for this one.

You'll end up with

  • Stripe connected to Google Calendar
  • Booking pages that take card payment before the booking goes through
  • A cancellation and refund policy on the booking form and in reminders
  • Automatic receipts, and a tested refund

You'll need

  • A paid Google plan that includes paid bookings, such as Google Workspace Business Standard. A free Google account can't take payment. On a company account an admin may also need to switch it on. The course checks yours.
  • A booking page in Google Calendar. No booking page yet? Start with Swap Calendly for Google Calendar bookings.
  • A Stripe account, or time to open one (Stripe runs ID checks).
  • Any AI chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. It goes at your pace, however much you know about Stripe.
  • No Stripe products needed: Google charges the amount you type in.

Daily email and calendar sweep

Start each day digging through the inbox to work out what's urgent? Let your AI do the first pass.

Every morning at 6am, your AI checks the inbox and calendar and sends you one short email: what needs a reply, what's on today, and what you're still waiting on. You start the day knowing what needs doing.

Get the instructions

Who it's for

You look after an inbox and calendar, your own or someone else's, and want to:

  • Know first thing what needs a reply, most urgent first
  • Spot clashes and missing meeting links before the day starts
  • Optionally, have draft replies waiting for you to check

Looking after someone else's inbox? You'll need access to it and their OK for AI to read it. The course checks this with you.

You'll end up with

  • One short email each morning, at a time you choose
  • What needs a reply, most urgent first
  • Today's meetings, with clashes and missing links flagged
  • Optional: draft replies waiting in your drafts folder

You'll need

  • Gmail and Google Calendar, or Outlook and Microsoft 365.
  • An AI tool that can run tasks on a schedule, such as Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude. This usually needs a paid plan, and the course checks yours.
  • No scheduling on your plan? The course sets it up as a one-click morning routine instead.
  • On a work account: your company's OK for AI to read email.

How it works

Three steps

1

Copy

Copy the instructions below, or download them as a file.

2

Paste

Paste them into a new chat in the AI tool you use for work.

3

Answer

Answer its questions one by one. It tests the sweep with you, then puts it on a schedule.

The sweep only reads. It never sends, deletes or replies to anything, and it only writes drafts if you ask it to.

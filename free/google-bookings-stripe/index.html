Swap Calendly for Google Calendar bookings

On Calendly's free plan, but need more than one type of booking, or want people to pay when they book? You don't have to upgrade. If you already pay for Google, your Google Calendar can do both.

Set up booking pages in the Google Calendar you already have, test them, move every old link across and switch off Calendly. Your AI chat coaches you through it one step at a time.

Get the instructions

Who it's for

You use Calendly on the free plan and want to:

  • Offer more than one type of booking, for example a 15-minute call and a 1-hour meeting
  • Take payment through Stripe before people book

Paying for Calendly already? If you're on Standard (around £8 a month at the time of writing), check which of its features you use before you switch, and make sure Google can do the same. The course goes through this with you.

You'll end up with

  • A booking link for each type of booking (for example a 15-minute video call or a 1-hour meeting at your office), ready to share or put on your website
  • Booking pages in Google Calendar
  • Automatic calendar invites with a Google Meet link, plus reminders
  • Your own step-by-step plan for moving every old link across, so you can cancel Calendly with confidence
  • Want people to pay when they book? Follow on with Connect Stripe and take payment before booking

You'll need

  • A paid Google account, such as Google Workspace, with the calendar you want people to book into. A free Google account can make a basic booking page, but you can't do as much. For example, you can't add more than one reminder or take payment through Stripe. The course checks your plan.
  • Any AI chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. The instructions below guide you step by step, and help if you get stuck.
  • For payments only: a Stripe account. You can set it up in the follow-on course.

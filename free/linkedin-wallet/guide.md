## Instructions for the AI

You are helping me put my LinkedIn business card, with its QR code, into the wallet app on my phone. Then, when I meet someone, I double-click the side button, show them the code, they scan it, and my LinkedIn profile opens. No searching for my name, no spelling it out.

This course is for anyone who meets people at events, meetings or over coffee and wants a quicker way to connect.

How to work with me:

1. **One step at a time.** Ask the questions in "Questions to ask first", one at a time. Then guide me through the steps one by one. Wait for me to say "done" (or tell you what I see) before the next step.
2. **I'm doing this on my phone.** Keep each message short enough to read on a phone screen. Tell me exactly what to tap.
3. **Screens change.** Button names and menus in the LinkedIn app change often. The steps below were right at the time of writing. If you can browse the web, check the official help pages (links below) before each step. If what I see doesn't match, ask me to describe the screen or send a screenshot, and work it out with me.
4. **Keep it safe.** Never ask for my LinkedIn password. I only use the official LinkedIn app, signed in as normal.
5. **Plain English, no jargon.**

## Reference: the steps

*Screen names were right at the time of writing (November 2026). Check the official help if anything looks different.*

### Step 1: Open your business card

- Open the LinkedIn app and tap your profile photo, top left. A menu slides out with your name and headline.
- Tap the QR code icon at the top of that menu.
- Your **business card** opens: your photo, name, headline and a QR code.

### Step 2: Choose how it looks (optional)

- Tap the pencil icon, top right, to edit the card.
- Pick a layout. For example, a dark card with your photo on the right stands out on a phone screen.
- Tap **Save**.
- Check it reads well: name, headline, company and location all visible, nothing cut off.

### Step 3: Add it to your wallet

- On iPhone, tap **Add to Apple Wallet** under the QR code.
- A preview of the pass appears. Tap **Add**, top right.
- On Android, follow what the app offers, or use the fallback in Step 5.

### Step 4: Practise showing it

- Lock your phone. Double-click the side button: Wallet opens. *(If it doesn't, the setting is in Settings → Wallet & Apple Pay → Double-Click Side Button.)*
- Swipe to the LinkedIn card if it isn't the first one.
- Test it: ask a colleague or a friend to scan the code with their phone camera. LinkedIn should open on your profile.

### Step 5: If there's no wallet option

- Take a screenshot of the business card screen and save it to a "Favourites" album, or
- Ask me to explain how to add a home-screen shortcut to the LinkedIn QR code on my phone.

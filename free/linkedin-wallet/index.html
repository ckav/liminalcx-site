Put your LinkedIn QR code in your wallet

Someone says "let's connect", and you're opening LinkedIn, searching for yourself and spelling out your name? Keep your code in your phone's wallet instead.

Double-click the side button, show your code, and they're on your profile in seconds. It takes five minutes to set up, and your AI can walk you through it and tidy up your headline while you're there.

See howGet the instructions

Who it's for

You meet people at events, meetings or over coffee, and want to:

  • Share your profile in seconds, without searching or spelling your name
  • Make sure people find the right you, not someone with the same name
  • Follow up with people who've already connected

On Android? Your business card and QR code are in the same place in the app. Whether you can save it to a wallet depends on your phone, so the course checks, and has a fallback if not.

You'll end up with

  • Your LinkedIn business card, with your photo, headline and QR code
  • The card saved in your wallet, ready from the lock screen
  • A layout you've chosen, that reads well on a phone
  • Optional: a sharper headline, so new contacts know what you do

You'll need

  • The LinkedIn app, up to date. The free account is fine.
  • An iPhone with Apple Wallet, which comes built in. Android: see the note in "Who it's for".
  • Any AI chat (optional): to walk you through it and suggest a shorter headline. The steps on this page work without it.

The course

Copy this into your AI chat

Copy and paste the text below into any AI tool, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, and it guides you step by step. It also checks your headline reads well on the card, and offers a shorter version if it doesn't.

Stuck? Ask it for help. A screenshot, or a description of what's on your screen, helps it guide you. The instructions include links to the official help pages, so if the app has changed, your AI can keep up.

What's a .md file? It's like a Notepad (.txt) file: plain text with no extra formatting, which makes it easy for AI tools to read.

Why together

See the whole journey, not just your part of it

People doing the work often see only their part of it. They improve their own bit, without seeing the opportunity or impact for employees or customers across the entire process.

When founders and teams look at the whole journey together, the friction shows up fast. Remove it, and people get back to meaningful work, with the freedom to keep improving it themselves.

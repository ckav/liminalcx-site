Who you'll work with

Claire Kavanagh

"The best technology disappears into the work. It doesn't add complexity. It removes it."

Claire Kavanagh, Liminal CX founder

I've spent over 20 years in the mobile industry and 15 years in start-ups, at the point where customer experience, technology and growth meet. I've been on the launch team of three mobile companies. I joined giffgaff before launch, the world's first 100% online mobile network, and became Head of Member Experience and a board member: I set up the CRM, member service and the community, and was accountable for half of all new members. As Customer Experience Director at The People's Operator, I helped launch the US business and worked with Jimmy Wales, the co-founder of Wikipedia, on its community. Most recently, as interim Global CMO of a new mobile business, I led platform selection and contract negotiations across four countries.

As a consultant, I've worked across many industries, including sport, eCommerce and media, with a spell as interim Head of Customer Service at a Premier League club. The pattern is the same every time: find where time and money leak, and fix the flow.

I've always been the marketer who could read the technical side. At university in the late 1990s I studied information systems alongside marketing, database and web design included, and that foundation has set me apart from other marketing leaders at my level ever since. For the last two years, AI has been part of how I deliver: I use it to turn meeting recordings into traceable requirements, to build working prototypes in days rather than weeks, and to check contracts line by line before anyone signs. The judgement stays with me and the client. The grind doesn't.

My view: you don't need another AI course. You already have the skills and the tools. This is about putting them to work with confidence and clarity, so you get consistent results, save time and make better business decisions.

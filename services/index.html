The approach

Stop learning. Start coaching.

Just like an intern, spend time establishing the ground rules and being clear on what you expect. Your AI tools will be more consistent, and you'll be able to delegate more.

The problem isn't learning how to use the tools. It's transferring your knowledge: your expertise, and "the way we do things around here", which is often hard to capture on paper.

In real life, that's coaching. So that's how we work: on your real tasks, with the tools you already have, until it's done your way.

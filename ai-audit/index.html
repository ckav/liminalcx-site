Plan: What we'll do

First hour

  1. Discovery: Audit the tools you already use, and spot the quick wins
  2. Quick customer journey: Map how you win business (biz dev), and mark what needs your attention
  3. Opportunity: Brief and build your PA, identifying tasks that can be launched now, and prioritising the next ones

Second hour

  1. Brief your intern: turn an existing chat into a brief, so that results are more consistent
  2. Build your board: Your "should do" list is never-ending. Identify what really needs your attention, and which mind gremlins can be parked.
  3. Create a handover document, so your action plan is clear for you and your AI tools.

Goal: You'll leave with

  • clarity on your AI strategy, and how you can protect your time (saving at least two hours a week is the goal)
  • a simple sketch of your biz dev process, identifying opportunities where AI can assist
  • onboarding lessons for your virtual PA, so they can sweep your inbox and calendar, tidy your desk at the end of the day and send you reminders
  • at least one job handed to your intern, so you never explain it twice
  • a customised 'one-pager' report from your board: what is the focus, what may need to stop to make room for it
  • your personalised instruction guide, that includes your action plan for you and your business so you can confidently continue on your own.
